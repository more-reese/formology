# Executive Summary (v0.4.0 — updated after collaborator review)

## Is formology additive? Modestly, and in two places.

1. **As a practice:** The four-step loop (take a case in its own language, render it as forms and contexts, compare at the level where cases become comparable, bring the finding back into the case's register) and the rule that the reader is a context. No field surveyed states these as such, though every field has pieces of them.

2. **As a vocabulary that unifies:** Hart's penumbra in law, Hollnagel's work-as-imagined vs. work-as-done in safety science, music theory's preference rules, and the reader-as-context in business documents all turn out to have the same shape. Formology gives one name to that shape. The POSIWID gap is a rename of Hollnagel's WAI/WAD (which has four varieties, not two). The three-way match in the PO case is a test of whether three conformations of one form agree — formology names what the match is *for*.

Everything else that looked new is a rename. The honest verdict is "a patchwork of renames with one or two additions," not "a rename of X" for any single X.

## What fell or changed (v0.4.0)

- **H3 fell** as an ontological claim ("context is not form"). Every context formology names is itself a form by H4's own test. The key signature occupies a slot, stands in for something recoverable, and means what it means only under Western notation. Confirmed by 8+ independent tracks. Survives only as role naming: "context" names a role in a resolution, not a kind of thing.
- **H6 fell** as stated. Withdrawing a context does not return the original proformation — it produces a new state carrying a trace. The erased key signature's accidentals still record the old key. Repeal a statute and accrued rights stand (UK Interpretation Act 1978 s.16). Confirmed by all six tracks.
- **H4 fell** as a single conjunctive test. It fails formology's own paradigm cases: the note on the staff, "issue," and the morpheme all fail condition (b). Replaced by a two-kind split: placeholders (anaphoric (b): pronouns, blank fields) and patterns (≥1 open parameter: types, open terms, "issue"). Excludes anchors (content from themselves) and limits (nothing to read).
- **H1** has a free parameter: which changes count as "the same thing changing" is itself the form. Depends on H2, not ground-floor.
- **H2** lost four qualifiers: "complete" (Hart's open texture), "undetermined" (Carnap's intension is complete), "declared" (registers are recognized, not declared), "one bit" (the pivot chord is doubly determined).
- **H5** keeps recursion, gains "no top" (Tarski, Girard), loses "no ground level" (four traditions have grounds).
- **H7** survives as a chosen posit (Fichte, Spencer-Brown) but treatise 12.2's self-confirmation is circular and 7.5 as written absorbs counter-evidence.

## The single most important prior-art match

A tie between two things that point the same way:

1. **Aristotle's hylomorphism with the labels swapped.** Formology's "form" is Aristotle's matter on the resolution axis (the determinable); formology's "context" is Aristotle's form (the determinant). The "two jobs" problem — "form" doing both invariant and substrate — is the source of the disjointness-vs-recursion problem and the form/proformation collapse suspicion.

2. **Model theory's interpretation of an open formula by a structure (Tarski/Carnap).** A formula with free variables is neither true nor false until a structure is given; supplying it is called interpretation. Under Carnap, proformation = intension (a function from states to extensions), context = a state, conformation = the extension at that state. This does everything H2 does and is cleaner about it. It also dissolves the disjointness-vs-recursion problem by type: intension and extension are different types.

## Recommended next step

Not more theory. Adopt the ending core (v0.4.0), replace the two disjoint classes (Proformation, Conformation) in the ontology with one relation `resolvedBy(form, context)`, and derive "level" as chain length. Then run the "issue" register case across four registers under the rewritten rules, hunting for one of the three practice-level disconfirmations that H7' names (register rejection, nesting failure, invariance failure). If none appears, that is the first [CONFIRMED] tag the framework would earn in practice rather than by consistency.
